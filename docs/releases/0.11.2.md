# PowerSpike 0.11.2

Blade of the Ruined King's tooltip now shows its current-health damage percentage for the selected champion instead of an unresolved placeholder.

- Melee and ranged percentages come from the selected patch's item data and share the calculation used by the combat simulation.
- The value includes its percent sign, and its formula explanation identifies the attack type and target-current-health basis.
- Equipped items, the item picker and both duel participants use their own scenario context. Existing cached patch records receive the fix without another download.

Regression checks cover melee and ranged champions on retained 16.18.1 and 16.19.1 item records, agreement with combat damage, preserved cached records and unresolved inputs. Local core, frontend, relocated-runtime and browser checks pass. The [GitHub packaging and browser workflow](https://github.com/tjisse/powerspike-janet/actions/runs/38085442150) and [signed RPM/DNF publication, installation and upgrade verification](https://github.com/tjisse/powerspike-janet/actions/runs/38085608960) succeeded. The release includes the signed x86_64 RPM and SHA-256 checksum; the signed DNF repository has been updated.
