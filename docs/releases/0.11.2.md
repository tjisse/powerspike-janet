# PowerSpike 0.11.2

Blade of the Ruined King's tooltip now shows its current-health damage percentage for the selected champion instead of an unresolved placeholder.

- Melee and ranged percentages come from the selected patch's item data and share the calculation used by the combat simulation.
- The value includes its percent sign, and its formula explanation identifies the attack type and target-current-health basis.
- Equipped items, the item picker and both duel participants use their own scenario context. Existing cached patch records receive the fix without another download.

Regression checks cover melee and ranged champions on retained 16.18.1 and 16.19.1 item records, agreement with combat damage, preserved cached records and unresolved inputs. Frontend and browser checks pass. Packaged release verification is pending.
