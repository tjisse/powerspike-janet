# PowerSpike 0.9.0

## Champion ability data

Choosing a champion or duel opponent whose abilities are absent from the initial seed automatically downloads full patch data. The current estimate stays visible during the download, and the completed patch opens with the current fight settings and inventories. Failure and cancellation show an explanation and allow a manual retry.

Saved, imported and shared scenarios retain their exact snapshots. Manual patch refreshes remain visible and continue polling when fight controls change. The download also covers an opponent with missing spell data when the player is Annie.

## Damage calculations and optimization

Completed items gain their source-derived damage effects: Spellblade for Lich Bane, Trinity Force, Essence Reaver and Iceborn Gauntlet; Nashor's Tooth on-hit damage; and Luden's Echo single-target damage. Formulas and cooldowns come from the selected patch. Remaining attack-speed, movement, mana, slow and multi-target omissions appear in coverage.

Viktor's Q now includes his empowered next attack, and R includes its periodic damage. Ahri's R supports its three casts and source-defined recast delay. Current semantics apply to retained records in existing cached snapshots without rewriting their files. Saved scenarios with an older combat model report the compatibility change.

The full-shop search explores stat multipliers together with penetration, addressing AP combinations that standalone item rankings missed. Scores still come from the combat simulation; champion roles and item completion do not add bonuses or restrict candidates.

The default practice target is now 2,500 HP rather than 10,000 HP. Its health remains editable and is preserved in saved scenarios. Burst includes spells, attacks and item effects over the configured window; very high-health targets can favor percentage-health attacks. The optimization dialog shows the current fight settings, and results display the gold limit captured when the search started.

## Verification and packaging

Regressions cover automatic downloads, pinned snapshots, manual refresh tracking, both duel participants, item formulas and cooldowns, mage spell effects, cached semantics, AP search results, gold caps and unbiased attack-only scoring. Browser verification checked Burst recommendations for Annie, Viktor and Ahri.

The release uses the existing signed x86_64 RPM and DNF repository workflow. Champion, item and objective coverage gaps remain explicit; modeled combat estimates are separate from in-game validation.
