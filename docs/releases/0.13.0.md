# PowerSpike 0.13.0

Replay verification now has a progress page at `/verification`, also available
under **Patch & evidence → Replay verification progress**. It tracks 27 combat
conditions, three stat-generation targets and 19 selected interaction pairs.
Coverage describes this catalogue; whole-game coverage and population accuracy
remain unmeasured. Checked coverage and model agreement are reported separately.

The offline workflow includes Riot replay access, exact-build decoding, partial
pre-hit state and buff recovery, gap audits and reviewed single-hit comparisons
through the production model. The retained two-game pilot contains 6,306
champion-to-champion damage events and 439 numerically ready ordinary-attack
candidates. Seven conditional stat checks and one damage diagnostic remain
provisional: **zero replay damage cases have passed admission for model
validation**. Attribution, effect context, sampling and shield semantics still
require review.

A versioned target catalogue and append-only private ledger retain case
identities, reviews, results, corrections and costs. The queue groups shared
blockers to prioritize useful coverage; diversity targets track games,
champions, encounters and conditions. Fresh-game reservations bind evaluations
to a frozen model and sampling policy. Model or catalogue changes do not inherit
old coverage silently, and mismatches remain in history.

The standalone executable embeds the aggregate progress snapshot. Operators can
supply a newer report through `PS_VERIFICATION_REPORT`; malformed configured
reports show evidence as unavailable. Raw replays, credentials, proprietary
decoder images and the private ledger are excluded from the release.

See the [operator workflow](../replay-coverage.md),
[coverage strategy](../replay-coverage-strategy.md) and
[replay investigation](../replay-verification.md). Automated replay acquisition,
semantic admission and comparison adapters beyond the current isolated-damage
path remain future work.

Local core, Python, optional exact-build decoder, frontend, browser and relocated
runtime checks passed. The [GitHub packaging and browser workflow](https://github.com/tjisse/powerspike-janet/actions/runs/39118875355)
and [signed RPM/DNF publication and installation workflow](https://github.com/tjisse/powerspike-janet/actions/runs/39119045366)
also passed. The signed repository verified a real version upgrade, preserved
configuration and cached patches, upgraded runtime behavior, reinstall and
removal. The downloaded release RPM matches its published SHA-256 checksum.
