# PowerSpike 0.11.1

Empowered basic attacks now use shared combat semantics. Yorick Q's bonus damage reaches the damage graph instead of being skipped when its parsed effect lacks an expiry.

- Additional damage preserves the ordinary physical attack and resolves separately using the bonus's damage type. Total attack damage replaces the ordinary hit.
- Empowerments support one attack, a source-defined attack count, or all attacks during a known buff window. Source spell tags identify attack resets.
- Recasts refresh the same empowerment; bonuses from separate abilities can coexist. Critical strikes affect ordinary attacks without multiplying unrelated bonuses, and split damage counts as one attack for on-hit triggers.
- Source-defined empowerment windows are distinguished from nearby slow and stun durations. When a finite attack count has no available expiry, the simulator reports its assumption. Timed buffs without a known window remain unresolved.
- Cached champion records receive the fix without rewriting snapshots or downloading the patch again. Tooltip combat interpretation is included in model identity.

Retained regression fixtures cover Yorick, Jax, Draven, Blitzcrank, Darius, Trundle, Leona, Xin Zhao and Master Yi. Parser and engine checks cover expiry, attack resets, multiple attacks, overlapping buffs, refreshes, critical strikes, mitigation and on-hit counting. Champion-specific stacking, forms, pets and conditional recasts still require explicit semantics; these checks are not in-game combat calibration.

Local core, frontend, relocated-runtime and browser checks pass. The rebuilt executable also reproduces Yorick Q damage against retained cached data. The [GitHub packaging and browser workflow](https://github.com/tjisse/powerspike-janet/actions/runs/38075061954) and [signed RPM/DNF publication, installation and upgrade verification](https://github.com/tjisse/powerspike-janet/actions/runs/38075251420) succeeded. The release includes the signed x86_64 RPM and SHA-256 checksum; the signed DNF repository has been updated.
